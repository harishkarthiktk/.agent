# 05: Subagent - CTA & Conversion

## Purpose
Evaluate the conclusion and call-to-action based on the article's specific intent (gathered in `01-gather-phase.md`).

## Evaluation Criteria
1.  **Intent Alignment:** What is a "strong" CTA for this piece? 
    *   *Opinion Piece:* A paradigm shift or thought-provoking question.
    *   *Proposal:* Clear next steps and objection handling.
    *   *Marketing:* A direct, low-friction action.
2.  **The Bridge:** Evaluate the transition from the body to the CTA. Does the pitch feel earned, or is it jarring and disconnected from the narrative?
3.  **Friction & Clarity:** Hunt for generic action text ("Click here", "Learn more"). Suggest value-driven alternatives ("Download the SEO Checklist").
4.  **Pacing:** Flag if the piece relies solely on one bottom-of-page CTA. Suggest a contextual, "soft" CTA mid-article for high-intent readers.
