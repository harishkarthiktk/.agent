# 03: Subagent - Narrative & Hook

## Purpose
Evaluate the story arc and reader engagement based on the framework selected during the `gather` phase.

## Evaluation Criteria
1.  **Dynamic Framework Application:** Map the article against the appropriate framework (e.g., PAS for problem-solving, AIDA for conversion, Inverted Pyramid for news). Identify missing components (e.g., jumping to the 'Solution' without enough 'Agitation').
2.  **Time-to-Value (The Hook):** Measure the first 150 words. Does the writer establish the stakes immediately? Flag anecdotal intros that lack a clear thesis or reason for the reader to care.
3.  **Output to Scratchpad:** List structural narrative gaps and specify where the hook is losing momentum.
