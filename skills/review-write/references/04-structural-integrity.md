# 04: Subagent - Structural Integrity

## Purpose
Evaluate the formatting, pacing, and tone consistency of the draft. Do not enforce rigid word counts; evaluate how the piece *feels* to read.

## Evaluation Criteria
1.  **Visual Rhythm & Cognitive Load:** Scan for "walls of text." Flag sections that require breaking up with subheads, bullets, or bolding. Ensure varying paragraph lengths to keep the reader's eye moving.
2.  **The Tone Curve:** Map the emotional shifts in the piece (e.g., empathetic intro -> authoritative body -> urgent conclusion). 
3.  **Accidental Deviations:** Flag jarring, accidental shifts in tone (e.g., slipping into rigid corporate jargon in the middle of a casual, empathetic story).
4.  **Output to Scratchpad:** Note exact line numbers where visual rhythm stalls or tone breaks.
