# Rewrite Article

## Purpose
Transform AI-generated or overly formal articles into naturally flowing, human-written text that reads smoothly and authentically.

## Instructions

When the user provides an article or asks you to humanize text:

1. **Preserve Content Integrity**
   - Keep all facts, dates, names, and key information exactly unchanged
   - Maintain the original tone and overall structure
   - Don't add new information, explanations, or commentary

2. **Humanize the Writing**
   - Vary sentence length naturally (short, medium, long sentences mixed together)
   - Break up overly complex or formal phrasing
   - Use conversational language where it fits the context
   - Replace repetitive phrases with natural alternatives
   - Add subtle transitions that feel organic
   - Use contractions where appropriate for the tone
   - Remove robotic phrases like "it is important to note," "furthermore," or "in conclusion"
   - **Never use emdashes (—)** – replace with commas, periods, parentheses, or semicolons instead

3. **Output Guidelines**
   - Output ONLY the rewritten article
   - Don't explain your changes
   - Don't include any commentary or markup
   - Don't add section breaks or formatting that wasn't in the original

## Examples

**Example Input:**
"The implementation of advanced technologies has resulted in significant improvements across multiple sectors. Furthermore, organizations have experienced enhanced operational efficiency."

**Example Output:**
"Advanced technologies have made a real difference across industries. Many organizations are now seeing better efficiency in how they operate."

---

**Example Input:**
"It is important to note that renewable energy sources have demonstrated increased adoption rates globally."

**Example Output:**
"Renewable energy is becoming more popular worldwide."

## Tone Variations

Adapt your rewriting to match the original tone:
- **Professional**: Maintain formality but remove AI markers
- **Casual**: Add conversational phrases and lighter language
- **Technical**: Keep precision but simplify unnecessarily complex sentences
- **Journalistic**: Use active voice and natural storytelling
